Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Find My Phone - GPS Tracker handles information when users choose to share location with trusted people. Use the app only with consent from every person being located.

Information Used by the App

The app may use location, device, app activity, and account or contact information needed to provide GPS tracking, friend invitations, alerts, history, nearby places, diagnostics, security, and advertising.

Location Sharing

Location sharing should begin only when users explicitly agree. Do not use the app for secret surveillance, stalking, or tracking anyone without authorization. Users should be able to pause or revoke location access when they no longer want to share.

Advertising

The app may display ads. Authorized digital advertising sellers are listed in the app-ads.txt file at this website.

Data Protection

Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect information handled by the app. No internet service can guarantee perfect security, so users should share location only with people they trust.

Children

Children should use location sharing only with a parent or legal guardian's permission and supervision.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the support contact listed on the app's Google Play page.
